FinTrack — 18 screens · mid-fidelity

One frame per kind of screen in the app: three signed-out, eight signed-in, the system states, the overlay set, and five modals. No variants, no repeats. Real copy and the demo account’s real figures (Maya Alvarez, late July 2026, computed by the app’s own selectors), real layout and real Lucide icons — still greyscale on purpose; colour, the neobrutalist borders and hard shadows arrive at high fidelity.

Magenta is annotation and is never part of the design — a dashed outline marks an interactive element, and its numbered badge matches a row in the legend printed under that frame. Numbering matches the low-fidelity 18-frame set.

Light grey fill — an accent surface (lime / yellow / pink / purple / sky in the real UI) Dark fill — the primary action on that screen Shaded row — involves the signed-in user Grey dashed — a not-configured / needs-credentials state Dashed magenta — an interactive element Numbered badge — matches the legend under the screen – instead of a number — described, but nothing to connect Mono caps note — a design note, not UI copy

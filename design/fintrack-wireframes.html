FinTrack — wireframes

Low-fidelity screens for the whole app, with the interaction layer drawn on. 28 boards, one page, nothing loaded from anywhere except the two webfonts.

Grey is the design. Magenta is annotation and is never part of it — a dashed outline marks an interactive element, and its numbered badge matches a row in the legend printed under that screen. Board 00 lists every connection in one place.

Flat mid-grey fill — an accent surface (lime / yellow / pink / purple / sky in the real UI) Grey bar — text that comes from data ₹ + bar — a computed figure Square with a diagonal — an icon slot Dashed magenta — an interactive element Numbered badge — matches the legend under the screen – instead of a number — described, but nothing to connect Grey dashed — a not-configured / needs-credentials state

FinTrack — 18 screens

One frame per kind of screen in the app: three signed-out, eight signed-in, the system states, the overlay set, and five modals. No variants, no repeats.

Grey is the design. Magenta is annotation and never part of it — a dashed outline marks an interactive element, and its numbered badge matches a row in the legend printed under that frame.

Flat mid-grey fill — an accent surface (lime / yellow / pink / purple / sky in the real UI) Grey bar — text that comes from data ₹ + bar — a computed figure Square with a diagonal — an icon slot Grey dashed — a not-configured state Dashed magenta — an interactive element Numbered badge — matches the legend under the frame – instead of a number — described, nothing to connect
Badges 1–6 mean the same thing on every signed-in frame — wire them once on a component
